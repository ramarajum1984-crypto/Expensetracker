Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Your financial data is personal. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a hashed version of your password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you choose to enter.
  • Session data — a session cookie that keeps you signed in while you use the app.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or payment information. Spendly never connects to your bank.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly, including to:

  • Create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • Store, display and summarise your expenses, such as category breakdowns and date-filtered views.
  • Respond to questions or support requests you send us.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in Spendly's database. Passwords are hashed before they are saved and are never stored in plain text. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual expenses at any time, and deleting your account removes your associated expense records.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests these fonts from Google's servers, which means Google may receive technical information such as your IP address. Google's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy.

We do not share your account or expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
